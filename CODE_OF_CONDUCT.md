# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want Cognito Profile Manager to be a welcoming, respectful project for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or point of view. People participating in this project are expected to help make it a safe and constructive space.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and respectful in discussions and reviews.
- Give feedback that focuses on ideas and work, not on personal traits.
- Welcome different experience levels and viewpoints.
- Accept reasonable correction and maintain a collaborative tone.
- Respect people's privacy and boundaries.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or unwanted sexual attention.
- Insults, trolling, or deliberately disruptive behavior.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional community.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, and project-related communications, as well as when someone is representing the project publicly.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report a conduct concern privately to the maintainer at [hi@lbeniciod.dev](mailto:hi@lbeniciod.dev). Include relevant links, dates, and context. Please do not report conduct concerns in a public issue.

The maintainer will review reports and respond in a way that is fair and proportionate. They may ask someone to stop a behavior, remove content, restrict participation temporarily, or permanently exclude someone from project spaces. Reports will be kept private as far as practical.
